China is also creating new pathways into the evolving labour market. In September, China officially recognised 11 new occupations and 23 new specialties within existing occupations, including embodied intelligence robot technician and AI agent developer–formalising new career paths emerging alongside technological change.


In September 2026, China’s Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (MOHRSS), alongside related state departments, officially published the **8th batch of new occupations**, formally adding **11 brand-new professions** (*新职业*).

*(Note: Roles like **AI Agent Developer** and **Low-Altitude Logistics Worker** were included in the same official announcement as part of the **23 new sub-specialties** [*新工种*] nested under broader existing occupational codes).*

The 11 officially recognized new occupations span digital transformation, green energy/decarbonization, advanced maritime/aerospace, and modern lifestyle services:

---

### 1. Digital & Frontier Technology

* **Embodied Intelligence Robot Application Technician** (*具身智能机器人应用技术员*)
Personnel operating teleoperation systems, spatial digitization platforms, and simulation environments to collect multimodal interaction data, adapt algorithms, and commission/maintain physical AI robots.
* **Digital Twin Engineering Technician** (*数字孪生工程技术人员*)
Engineers responsible for building high-fidelity virtual models of physical assets, industrial systems, and urban environments to enable real-time simulation, monitoring, and predictive optimization.
* **Industrial Product Digital Modeling Specialist** (*工业产品数字建模师*)
Designers leveraging 3D parametric modeling, reverse engineering, and generative digital design tools to conceptualize and optimize manufactured components.
* **Sports Data Analyst** (*运动数据分析师*)
Specialists using computer vision, biometric sensors, and predictive analytics to track athletic performance, design training regimens, and assist tactical game strategy.

### 2. Green Energy & Carbon Neutrality

* **Microgrid Administrator** (*微电网管理员*)
*(Dual-classified as both a green and digital profession)* Managers overseeing localized, decentralized power grids integrating distributed solar/wind generation, battery energy storage systems, and EV chargers.
* **Hydrogen Fuel Cell Manufacturer** (*氢燃料电池制造工*)
Specialized technicians handling membrane electrode assembly, bipolar plate stacking, sealing, and testing for commercial hydrogen fuel cell power systems.
* **Water Electrolysis Hydrogen Production Worker** (*电解水制氢工*)
Operators managing industrial electrolyzer arrays (PEM, alkaline, or AEM) to produce green hydrogen from renewable electrical inputs.
* **Corporate Sustainability Planner** (*企业可持续发展规划师*)
Strategic specialists responsible for carbon accounting, ESG compliance, life-cycle emissions assessments, and circular economy roadmaps across enterprise supply chains.

### 3. Advanced Engineering, Marine & Aerospace

* **Shore-Based Vessel Management Engineering Technician** (*船舶岸基管理工程技术人员*)
Engineers stationed at shore facilities utilizing maritime telemetry, satellite IoT, and digital fleet management tools to monitor, route, and support autonomous or semi-autonomous marine vessels.
* **Satellite Navigation Equipment Assembler and Calibrator** (*卫星导航设备装调工*)
Technicians assembling, tuning, and calibrating terminals, base stations, and high-precision antenna arrays compatible with the BeiDou Navigation Satellite System (BDS).

### 4. Cultural & Consumer Services

* **Incense Artisan / Aromatic Craftsman** (*制香香艺师*)
Specialists blending traditional Chinese herbal formulas, natural aromatics, and modern scent formulation to produce heritage incense and olfactory products catering to wellness and traditional culture markets.

---

### Context: What Recognition Actually Triggers

In China’s labor system, inclusion on this list is not just symbolic:

* **National Vocational Standards:** MOHRSS formulates formal tier-based qualification tests (Levels 1–5, from Junior Technician to Senior Master).
* **Vocational Curriculum Subsidies:** Vocational schools and technical colleges (*职业技术学院*) gain government authorization and subsidies to open corresponding degree tracks and vocational programs.
* **Municipal Hiring Incentives:** Cities (such as Shenzhen, Shanghai, and Suzhou) link household registration (*Hukou*), housing subsidies, and training credits to workers certified under these designated national occupations.

* --
* The **23 new specialties** (*新工种*) officially added under existing occupational categories represent operational specializations created by emerging industries—particularly the **low-altitude economy, AI software agents, new energy vehicles, silver economy, and specialized consumer services**.

In China's occupational classification system, a *specialty* (*工种*) is a designated sub-branch under an established broad occupation, allowing vocational schools and certification bodies to issue targeted skill credentials.

---

### Core Areas of the 23 New Specialties

#### 1. Artificial Intelligence & Next-Gen Software

* **AI Agent Developer** (*智能体开发员*)
Engineers designing autonomous LLM-driven workflows, multi-agent frameworks, tool-use integrations, and enterprise automated workflows.

#### 2. The Low-Altitude Economy & Aviation Support

* **Low-Altitude Logistics Worker** (*低空物流员*)
Operators managing drone delivery networks, automated flight path ground dispatches, and drone cargo loading/unloading terminals.
* **Low-Altitude Intelligent Connected System Administrator** (*低空智能网联系统管理员*)
Systems technicians managing UTM (Unmanned Traffic Management) telemetry, 5G-A network connectivity, and airspace tracking for unmanned aircraft fleets.
* **Airport Obstacle Clearance Inspector** (*机场净空巡查员*)
Specialists inspecting and safeguarding terminal airspace boundaries, glide paths, and flight protection corridors against physical obstacles, unauthorized drones, and laser interference.

#### 3. New Energy Vehicles (NEVs) & Smart Mobility

* **New Energy Vehicle Inspector / Tester** (*新能源汽车检测员*)
Technicians conducting safety diagnostics on high-voltage traction batteries, battery management systems (BMS), electric drive motors, and automotive edge control units.

#### 4. Silver Economy & Age-Friendly Infrastructure

* **Age-Friendly Renovation Designer** (*适老化改造设计师*)
Interior and environmental designers retrofitting home and community spaces for elderly residents (e.g., barrier-free navigation, fall-prevention mechanics, and ambient health-monitoring hardware).

#### 5. Urban Governance & Modern Consumer Services

* **Community Worker** (*社区工作者*)
Professionalized grassroots public service specialists coordinating neighborhood digital governance, elderly welfare, and community dispute mediation.
* **Fresh Cut Flower Processing Worker** (*鲜切花加工工*)
Post-harvest floriculture specialists managing cold-chain sorting, preservation treatments, and commercial floral packaging.
* **Ice and Snow Sculptor** (*冰雪雕塑工*)
Artisans and structural sculptors supporting regional winter tourism, ice-snow cultural festivals, and themed exhibitions.

*(The remaining specialties across the batch include related sub-disciplines in advanced green manufacturing inspection, precision electronics assembly, modern logistics sorting, and agricultural commodity brokerage).*

---

### Strategic Significance of Adding Specialties

* **Fast-Tracking Without New Legislation:** Creating a completely new occupational code (*新职业*) requires extensive cross-ministerial restructuring, whereas adding a specialty (*新工种*) allows the government to quickly update existing vocational certification standards.
* **Alignment with Strategic Policy:** The additions directly align with national industrial priorities—formalizing roles for the **low-altitude economy** (commercial drones), **AI agents**, and the **aging society** (eldercare and retrofitting) within standard municipal training subsidies and apprenticeship programs.
