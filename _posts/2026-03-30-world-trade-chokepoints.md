
Here are the 7 key choke points discussed:

Bab el-Mandeb (0:47): Located at the entrance to the Red Sea. Houthi attacks have forced many ships to avoid this route, adding 6,000 miles and nearly two weeks to journeys (0:52-1:01).
Suez Canal (1:11): Links Europe and Asia. A blockage here, like the Ever Given in 2021, can halt billions of dollars in trade daily (1:17-1:24).
Strait of Hormuz (1:38): Carries roughly 20% of the world's oil and gas. Conflict around Iran has reduced traffic, driving up fuel prices (1:40-1:59).
Strait of Malacca (2:02): Asia's largest choke point, linking the Indian Ocean to the Pacific. It is fragile due to heavy traffic and piracy risks (2:04-2:16).
Danish Straits (2:28): A key, shallow route for Russian oil exports (2:29-2:36).
Turkish Straits (2:39): The only gateway for Black Sea exports, hazardous due to narrow lanes and currents (2:40-2:48).
Panama Canal (2:56): Links the Atlantic and Pacific oceans. Drought conditions have recently caused delays and higher shipping costs (2:59-3:05).
